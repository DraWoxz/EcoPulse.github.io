01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Environmental problems are connected.

Climate, water, biodiversity, energy and waste are not separate boxes. A decision in one system can create consequences somewhere else.

01

Resources

Everything we build begins with materials, energy and land.

02

Production

Products require manufacturing, transport, packaging and infrastructure.

03

Consumption

Our everyday choices influence demand for energy, food and materials.

04

Waste

When products are discarded, resources can be lost unless systems recover them.

05

Impact

Pollution, emissions and ecosystem pressure can affect communities and nature.

🧠 KEY IDEASustainability is not about one perfect choice. It is about designing systems where better choices become easier, affordable and normal.

      03 / SEE THE PROBLEM

      Four pressures shaping our future.

      01

      Plastic Pollution

      Plastic can travel through drainage systems and rivers into marine environments. Wildlife can be harmed and larger items can fragment into smaller particles.

      IMPACT Ecosystems • wildlife • waste systems
      → Reduce • Reuse • Recover
      02

      Climate Change

      Increasing greenhouse-gas concentrations warm the climate system and can shift risks involving heat, rainfall, oceans and ecosystems.

      IMPACT Food • water • health • infrastructure
      → Efficiency • clean energy • resilience
      03

      Deforestation

      Forest loss can remove habitat, release stored carbon, increase soil erosion and alter water cycles.

      IMPACT Biodiversity • carbon • soils • water
      → Protect • restore • use land wisely
      04

      E-Waste

      Discarded electronics contain useful materials but may require controlled collection and processing.

      IMPACT Resources • pollution • material loss
      → Repair • reuse • responsible recycling
      LOOK CLOSER

      What happens when nature loses its room to breathe?

      Healthy ecosystems provide services we often notice only after they are damaged: clean water, fertile soils, habitat, carbon storage and protection from environmental stress.

      04 / BUILD THE SOLUTION

      Six levers for a more sustainable future.

      01

      Reduce

      Prevent unnecessary consumption and wasted energy before dealing with the waste afterward.

      02

      Reuse

      Keep useful products and materials in circulation for as long as practical.

      03

      Repair

      Extending product life reduces the need for new materials and manufacturing.

      04

      Restore

      Protect and recover forests, wetlands, rivers and habitats with science-based approaches.

      05

      Innovate

      Use technology, design and infrastructure to make lower-impact choices easier.

      06

      Collaborate

      Schools, communities, businesses and governments all influence environmental outcomes.
